Section 302IPC
Punishment for murder
Plain English:
Intentional killing is murder. It is the most serious crime against the human body. The punishment is either life imprisonment or the death penalty.
"Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine."Official Source

Section 307IPC
Attempt to murder
Plain English:
Doing an act with the intention to kill, but failing to cause death. If hurt is caused, the penalty increases.
"Whoever does any act with such intention or knowledge... shall be punished with imprisonment... up to ten years..."

Section 376IPC
Punishment for rape
Plain English:
Sexual intercourse without consent. The law is very strict, prescribing minimum 10 years rigorous imprisonment up to life.
"Whoever... commits rape, shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment... for a term which shall not be less than ten years..."
Official Source

Section 304BIPC
Dowry Death
Plain English:
Where a woman dies unnaturally within 7 years of marriage, and it is shown she was harassed for dowry soon before death. Minimum punishment is 7 years imprisonment.
"Where the death of a woman is caused by burns or bodily injury or occurs otherwise than under normal circumstances within seven years of her marriage..."
Official Source

Section 420IPC
Cheating & Fraud
Plain English:
Deceiving someone to get them to hand over property. Commonly known as "420 fraud." Punishment up to 7 years.
"Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property..."

Section 125CrPC
Maintenance
Plain English:
A magistrate can order a person to pay monthly allowance for the maintenance of their wife, children, or parents if they are neglected.
"If any person... neglects or refuses to maintain... his wife... minor child... or parents..."
